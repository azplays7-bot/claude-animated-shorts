# How banks make money using your money 💰

A 60 s vertical explainer (720x1280, 30 fps) for ages 12–14, split into three parts for editing. Everything is
generated by code: 3D scenes (three.js in Remotion), AI narration (Kokoro TTS, voice `am_puck`, offline), synced
subtitles, and a synthesized music track and sound effects. No stock footage, samples or paid services.

## The parts
Cut at scene boundaries, in the pause between two sentences, so each part starts and ends cleanly:

| Part | Scenes | Time in the full video |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook, deposits → loans | 0.00 – 14.90 s |
| 2 | Interest, your savings vs the bank's loan | 14.90 – 34.93 s |
| 3 | Investments & fees, the big reveal | 34.93 – 60.00 s |

The narration takes about 58.7 s at a natural pace, so the scenes follow the voice instead of fixed 10 s blocks.

## Rebuild
```bash
npm install
pip install kokoro-onnx soundfile scipy
python narration/make_narration.py kokoro-v1.0.onnx voices-v1.0.bin   # narration.wav, timing.json, captions.srt
python audio/make_audio.py                                             # audio/mix.wav (-14 LUFS, exactly 60 s)
node render.mjs '{"mode":"render","comp":"Banks60","gl":"swangle","chromeMode":"headless-shell","scale":1,"from":0,"to":1799,"chunk":60,"out":"out/chunks","concurrency":1,"crf":17}'
python make_parts.py                                                   # out/part1-3.mp4 + .srt, out/full_60s.mp4
```
To change a line: edit `narration/script.json` (`say` = what the voice reads, `cap` = the subtitle), then re-run
everything. The scenes, subtitles and sound effects all follow `narration/timing.json`.

## Notes on accuracy
- The 8% loan and 1% savings rates are simplified examples, labelled as such on screen. They are not real bank rates.
- Mutual funds are shown as a product customers **choose** to buy, with the bank charging a fee for helping. They are
  not shown as something the bank does with customers' deposits.
- Each term gets a one-line everyday explanation: deposit, loan, interest, savings account, government bond, mutual
  fund, expenses and losses.

## Files
| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `narration/script.json` | The script: what the voice says and what the subtitle shows, chunk by chunk |
| `narration/make_narration.py` | TTS, sentence layout on the 60 s timeline, subtitle timing, `captions.srt` |
| `src/timeline.ts` | Scene windows and parts (derived from the narration), palette, character looks |
| `src/Scenes.tsx` | Lobby, street (bank, house, government), room, split screen, investing desk, city |
| `src/Actors.tsx` | The teen and the other characters, coins, bills |
| `src/Overlay.tsx` | Headlines, term cards, interest math, phone screen, subtitles, call to action |
| `audio/make_audio.py` | Music, sound effects and the mix |
