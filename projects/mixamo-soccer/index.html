    Get your files from Mixamo on iPad

    1. Open mixamo.com in Safari and sign in with your free Adobe account. If the site looks cramped, tap aA › Request Desktop Website.
    2. Pick or upload your character, then tap Download: Format FBX Binary (.fbx), Skin With Skin, Pose T-pose. This is your character file.
    3. Search animations for Running (or “Sprint”, “Jog Forward”). Tick In Place, then Download with Skin Without Skin, Frames per Second 30. If you forget In Place, the page removes the forward drift for you.
    4. Safari saves both into Files › Downloads. Come back here, tap Choose files…, open Downloads, tap Select, pick both files, and tap Open.
    5. The scene rebuilds with your character. Use Run speed if the feet slide, and Character height to make him a boy or a teenager.

    Your files stay on your iPad: they are read in this page, never uploaded anywhere. The page remembers them on this device so they reload next time.
