## Voice-over (one steady tone, ~240 words ≈ 65 s at 3.7 words/s)
1. **Hook:** The most famous tower in Italy started falling over before it was even finished.
2. **Setting:** Pisa, 1173. The city is rich, proud, and building a bell tower to show it.
3. **Action 1:** Five years in, the builders step back, and notice something. It's leaning.
4. One side is standing on soft clay. It's like building a tower of blocks on a mattress.
5. **Action 2:** Then war breaks out, and the work stops. For almost a hundred years.
6. **Deadpan double:** Luckily, the long pause let the ground settle and get stronger. Unluckily, the tower was still crooked.
7. **Action 3:** So the next builders cheated. They made every new floor a little taller on the short side, to bend the tower back up. That's why it isn't straight today. It's slightly banana-shaped.
8. **Action 4:** In 1838, an architect dug around the base to show off the steps. Water poured in. It leaned more.
9. In 1934, Mussolini wanted it straight. Engineers pumped concrete into the foundation. It leaned more.
10. **Explanation:** Picture a stack of books on a soft bed. Press on the low side, and it only sinks deeper.
11. **Snap:** By 1990, the tower was declared unsafe, and the doors were shut.
12. **Consequence:** So engineers did the opposite. Under the high side, they pulled out tiny scoops of soil, a little at a time, and the tower slowly leaned back.
13. **Callback twist:** But they stopped long before it was straight. Because in Pisa, a tower that stands up straight is just a tower.

## Visual beats (for the shot plan)
- **Hook:** low golden-hour shot of a cartoon builder holding a plumb line that swings away from the wall. Snap white flash.
- **1173:** day light. A bustling square, scaffolding and carts. Proud mayor and stonemason characters.
- **Lean reveal:** a wide shot whose roll matches the lean as the builders tilt their heads.
- **Mattress analogy:** a kid's block tower on a bed, sinking.
- **War pause:** builders leave, grass grows, seasons pass (crossfade). Callback: the same builders' great-grandchildren return.
- **Banana floors:** the camera cranes up the tower as new floors grow taller on one side.
- **1838 and 1934:** quick cuts, each ending on the same tilt "kick".
- **Books on a bed:** an insert.
- **1990:** night, a CLOSED barrier (no text on screen: just the barrier and a padlock).
- **The fix:** a cut-away of the ground, with tiny scoops of soil drawn out by augers.
- **Ending:** day light, tourists doing the "holding up the tower" pose. Callback to the hook's plumb line.
