# The Great Emu War (Western Australia, 1932)

A 60 s vertical short in the detailed style. Narration: Kokoro `am_fenrir`, speed 1.15.

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| 1932, Western Australia: soldiers with machine guns sent against emus; the emus came out ahead | Britannica, National Geographic, History Hit |
| Many farmers were WWI veterans; emus damaged their wheat | Wikipedia, National Geographic |
| Major G.P.W. Meredith, two soldiers, two Lewis guns, 10,000 rounds | Wikipedia, History Hit, Scientific American |
| Began in November 1932 near Campion | Wikipedia, Britannica |
| Emus scattered; a gun jammed | Wikipedia, Mental Floss |
| Shooting from a truck failed on rough ground | Wikipedia, History Hit |
| About 2,500 rounds fired by about Nov 8; reports of as few as about 50 birds (others say about 200) | Wikipedia, Britannica |
| Ornithologist Dominic Serventy: emus used "guerrilla tactics" | Wikipedia (quoting Serventy) |
| Withdrawn, redeployed, then ended | Wikipedia, Britannica |
| Meredith claimed 986 kills for about 9,860 rounds (about 10 per bird) | Wikipedia, History Hit |

The often-repeated "20,000 emus" is left out because it could not be confirmed. Kill counts differ between sources.

Code: `toon/src/emu` (composition `EmuShort`) plus the shared `toon/src/look`. Audio: `audio/make_audio.py`. Narration: `narration/`.
