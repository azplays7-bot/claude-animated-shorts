# What if everyone withdrew their money from the bank?

A 60 s vertical explainer for ages 10–13, at 1080x1920 and 30 fps. Everything is generated by code:
- **Scenes:** 3D in three.js inside Remotion, designed at 720x1280 and rendered at scale 1.5.
- **Narration:** AI voice (Kokoro TTS, `am_puck`, offline).
- **Subtitles:** synced to the narration.
- **Sound:** a synthesized score and sound effects.

Built on the shared kit from `projects/banks60` (`src/Kit.tsx`).

| Time | Scene |
|---|---|
| 0 – 8.9 s | The big question: a sunny city, and the line outside the bank grows to hundreds |
| 8.9 – 19.9 s | Where your money goes: a $1,000 deposit, the vault, most of it lent out as home and car loans |
| 19.9 – 28.3 s | The problem: a crowded lobby, worried tellers, the vault's cash draining, "BANK RUN!" |
| 28.3 – 36.6 s | The domino effect: the line outside, a quick sale at a loss, bank dominoes falling |
| 36.6 – 49.4 s | Protection: a friendly employee, an FDIC shield around a piggy bank, "FDIC INSURANCE — UP TO $250,000" |
| 49.4 – 60 s | Calm returns: the line breaks up, then safety systems and "FOLLOW FOR MORE CRAZY FACTS!" |

Scene starts aim at the brief's 10 s blocks, as far as the narration allows. The FDIC sentence alone takes about 11 s of
speech, so scenes 4–6 shift slightly.

## Accuracy notes
- The FDIC line follows the FDIC's standard wording: $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, for each account
  ownership category. The shield is a generic shield with the letters FDIC, not the agency's official logo.
- The "kept ready vs lent out" split and the $100 → $80 quick sale are illustrations, and are labelled as such on screen.
- The vault design takes its inspiration (round steel door, crates of cash bundles, gold bars, lantern) from a reference
  image the user supplied. Nothing from that image is copied.

## Rebuild
```bash
npm install            # or reuse ../banks60/node_modules
python narration/make_narration.py kokoro-v1.0.onnx voices-v1.0.bin
python audio/make_audio.py
node render.mjs '{"mode":"render","comp":"BankRun60","gl":"swangle","chromeMode":"headless-shell","scale":1.5,"from":0,"to":1799,"chunk":60,"out":"out/chunks","concurrency":1,"crf":17}'
python make_video.py   # out/bank_run_60s.mp4 + .srt
```
