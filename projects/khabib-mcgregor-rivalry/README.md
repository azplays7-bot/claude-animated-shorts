# Khabib vs McGregor: how the rivalry started (3 parts, work in progress)

60 s vertical cartoon shorts in the reference style: long gliding shots, close framing, rim light, and glowing screens. Narrated by am_fenrir (Kokoro, speed 1.15) and rendered at 60 fps.

- Part 1, "Friends to enemies": 2014 friendly messages, 2016 title-shot snub, backstage weigh-in clash, Conor's two belts, Lobov's interview on Russian TV.
- Part 2, "The bus" (April 2018): the Lobov confrontation, the loading-dock bus attack, injured fighters and cancelled bouts, the guilty plea, Khabib winning the title.
- Part 3, "Las Vegas" (Oct 6, 2018): the press conference, the neck-crank win, the brawl, suspensions and fines, retirement at 29-0.

Violence is kept comic and off-screen (no blood, and no landed blows shown up close). The characters are caricatures.

Code is in `toon/src/mma` (compositions `Mma1Short`..`Mma3Short`) plus the shared `toon/src/look`. Audio is made by `audio/make_audio.py <part>`. Narration is in `partN/narration/`.
