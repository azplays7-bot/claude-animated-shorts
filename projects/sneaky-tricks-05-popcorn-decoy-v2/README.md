# Sneaky Business Tricks #5 (v2): the medium popcorn decoy, detailed style

The same script, narration, music and facts as `sneaky-tricks-05-popcorn-decoy`, rebuilt in the detailed style from the Tony's counter look test (`look-test-tonys-counter`).

- **Locations:** the street (STARLIGHT cinema with marquee and popcorn cart, Tony's storefront with an LED sign board, a bookshop, a laundromat, a road, lamps, trees and a car), Tony's place (counter, kitchen line, dining area, wall TV), and the street again at sunset.
- **Facts on screens and signs in the scene:**
  - The popcorn board: rows highlight as they are read, the medium row slides in, plus "WHAT A STEAL", "ONLY $1 MORE", "NOT REALLY FOR SALE" and the DECOY stamp.
  - The TV: the classroom magazine test (web only $60 / print + web $125: 68% / 32%; with print-only $125 added: 16% / 0% / 84%), then "SMALL TEST · 1 CLASSROOM", "DECOYS DON'T ALWAYS WORK" and the example board.
  - Tony's menu screen and register.
  - Tony's LED board ("PART 5: THE END", "FOLLOW FOR MORE...").
- **Cast:** Tony, Max, a popcorn seller, and students Lila, Gus and Ray, all with outlines, faces that blink and talk, costumes and props.
- **Code:** `toon/src/dec2` (composition `Dec2Short`) and the shared `toon/src/look` (helpers, characters, Tony's set).

Sources are the same as Part 5 (see that project's README).
