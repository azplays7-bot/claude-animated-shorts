# The 1904 Olympic Marathon (St. Louis)

A 60 s vertical short in the detailed style. Narration: Kokoro `am_fenrir`, speed 1.15.

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Run in St. Louis in heat of about 90°F on dusty, unpaved roads; cars kicked up dust | Wikipedia, Smithsonian, Olympics.com |
| Only two water sources: a water tower at mile 6 and a well near mile 12 | Wikipedia (men's marathon article) |
| 32 starters, 14 finishers | Wikipedia, Olympedia, Smithsonian |
| Fred Lorz quit around mile 9, rode in a car until it broke down near mile 19, crossed first, and was banned | Wikipedia, Olympics.com, Smithsonian |
| Lorz was reinstated and won the Boston Marathon in 1905 | Wikipedia |
| Thomas Hicks was given strychnine and brandy (and raw egg) by his trainers and finished hallucinating | Wikipedia, Olympics.com, Smithsonian, Popular Science |
| Winning time about 3:28, the slowest Olympic marathon winning time | Wikipedia, Olympedia |
| Félix Carvajal (Cuban mailman) ran in street clothes, ate rotten apples, napped, and finished 4th | Wikipedia, Smithsonian, Olympedia |
| Len Taunyane was chased about a mile off course by wild dogs | Wikipedia, Olympics.com |
| William Garcia nearly died after inhaling dust | Wikipedia, Olympics.com |
