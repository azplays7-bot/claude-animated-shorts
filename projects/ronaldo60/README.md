# The Boy Nobody Believed In

A 60 s vertical cinematic 3D documentary short (1080x1920, 30 fps) about Cristiano Ronaldo's publicly reported early life.
Everything is generated by code:
- 3D scenes in three.js inside Remotion, using a procedural, faceless human figure (`src/Human.tsx`).
- AI narration with Kokoro `am_michael`.
- Captions with yellow keywords.
- A synthesized orchestral-style score and sound effects (`audio/make_audio.py`).

**Likeness:** no real person's face is rendered at any point.
- The boy and teenager are stylised and faceless, shown from behind or in profile.
- The adult is a backlit black silhouette.
- The reveal at 55.85 s is by name only: "CRISTIANO RONALDO" in gold.
- There are no club crests, real kits or shirt numbers.

| 19.9–30.9 | The sacrifice | Leaving at 12 (airport, family behind the fence); academy dorm, homesick, mocked accent; boots laced |
| 30.9–41.9 | The setback | Hospital at 15, ECG monitor, heart condition; recovery, slow-motion then full-speed sprint |
| 41.9–54.3 | The rise | Goal, crowd, trophy, award, ending alone in an empty floodlit stadium |
| 54.3–60 | The reveal | Music stops; the silhouette turns; crowd erupts; name in gold; final message |

## Rebuild
1. `python narration/make_narration.py`
2. `python audio/make_audio.py`
3. Render with `render.mjs`, using `"comp":"Ronaldo60","scale":1.5`.
4. `python make_video.py`
