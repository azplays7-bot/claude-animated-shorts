# Your Life Could Be Completely Different in 10 Years

A 60 s vertical (1080x1920, 30 fps) motivational explainer for teens and young adults about starting small: saving,
investing consistently and building a small business, with honest setbacks and disclaimers. Everything is generated by
code: the 3D scenes (three.js in Remotion, shared kit from `banks60`), the AI narration (Kokoro `am_puck`), captions with
yellow keywords, and an original score from piano to cinematic, plus sound effects.

**The numbers:** $100 a month for 10 years = $12,000 put in. At a hypothetical 7% average yearly return, compounded
monthly: about $1,239 after year 1, $7,159 after year 5 and **$17,308** after year 10. The formula is in
`src/Scenes.tsx` (`fv`). On screen they're labelled hypothetical, not guaranteed, before taxes and fees.

**Daniel:** the same character model throughout. At 18: short dark hair, red hoodie, black jeans, white sneakers. He
ages to 28 during the montage (`danielAt(age)`): stubble grows, his build fills out a little, and the hoodie becomes a
red work jacket.

`SCENE_PROMPTS.md` has six AI-video prompts for regenerating the story with an external tool.

Rebuild: `python narration/make_narration.py …`, `python audio/make_audio.py`, render with `render.mjs` at
`"scale":1.5`, then `python make_video.py`.
