# Your Life Could Be Completely Different in 10 Years: six 10-second AI video prompts

Use these to regenerate the story with an AI video tool (Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0, Veo, Runway). The rendered version in
`out/ten_years_60s.mp4` was made in code from this same plan. Narration (`narration/narration.wav`) and captions
(`narration/captions.srt`) are timed to these scenes.

**Character lock (attach the same reference images to every scene):**
- **DANIEL at 18:** short dark-brown hair, brown eyes, light-tan skin, slim build, red hoodie, black jeans, white sneakers.
- **DANIEL at 28:** the same face and hair with a short trimmed beard, slightly fuller build, red work jacket over a dark
  T-shirt, black jeans, white sneakers.
- **Everyone else:** muted colours, so Daniel's red always stands out.

**Style lock (append to every prompt):** stylized 3D cartoon animation, feature-film quality, expressive faces, soft
global illumination, warm cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, smooth natural motion, vertical 9:16, 1080x1920,
30 fps. **Negative:** text, logos, watermarks, morphing faces, extra fingers, flicker, slideshow, static zoom.

### Scene 1 (0–8 s): Two possible futures
**Narration:** "Ten years from now, your life could look completely different. And one of the biggest reasons might be what you decide to do with your money today."
**Prompt:** Evening, DANIEL (18) sits on his bed in a small bedroom, scrolling his phone, blue screen light on his face. He blinks and looks up thoughtfully. A glowing golden clock materializes in front of him and its hands spin faster and faster (10 years passing), particles swirling. Whip transition into a split screen divided by a glowing gold line. Left: DANIEL (28), cold blue light, sitting with overdue bills and an empty savings jar, worried. Right: DANIEL (28), warm golden light, smiling, waving in front of a white van with a red stripe, a full savings jar beside him.
**Camera:** slow push-in on Daniel, then a spin-zoom into the clock, then a locked symmetrical split-screen wide shot.
**SFX:** clock ticking, a whoosh, a soft boom on the split.
**On-screen:** "YOUR LIFE IN 10 YEARS".

### Scene 2 (8–17 s): Starting small
**Narration:** "You don't need to be rich to begin. Imagine saving just a hundred dollars each month, while learning how to start a small business with skills you already have."
**Prompt:** DANIEL (18) opens a paycheck envelope in his bedroom, smiles, and slides one $100 bill into a glass jar labelled SAVINGS. Cut to him at his desk at night, typing on a laptop next to books about investing and small business, nodding as he learns. Cut to his parents' driveway in daylight: he sets down a blue bucket, a sponge and a hose, and plants a hand-painted sign advertising car cleaning.
**Camera:** close-up on the hands and jar, a side profile at the desk, a wide tracking shot in the driveway.
**SFX:** paper rustle, coin clink, keyboard typing, a birdsong ambience.
**On-screen:** "START SMALL. START LEARNING."

### Scene 3 (17–28 s): The power of investing
**Prompt:** DANIEL (18) taps his phone; a glowing coin drops every month into a woven basket full of tiny cubes showing different company icons (a diversified, low-cost index fund). Transition into a dark studio where a 3D bar chart grows: ten blue bars (money put in, $1,200 per year) with a glowing green line rising above them (the account value). Daniel watches from the side, amazed.
**Camera:** orbit around the basket, then a slow dolly along the growing chart that rises to follow the green line.
**SFX:** soft coin clinks on each deposit, a gentle "pop" at years 1, 5 and 10, a cash register at the end.
**On-screen (accurate numbers, $100/month at a hypothetical 7% yearly return, compounded monthly):** Year 1: $1,200 put in, ≈ $1,239. Year 5: $6,000, ≈ $7,159. Year 10: $12,000, ≈ $17,308. "$12,000 INVESTED → ABOUT $17,300". **Disclaimer:** "Hypothetical 7% average annual return, compounded monthly. Not guaranteed. Before taxes and fees. Real returns go up and down."

### Scene 4 (28–38 s): Building a business
**Prompt:** DANIEL (18) scrubs a blue sedan covered in suds while a neighbour waits, then the neighbour hands him a $50 bill and Daniel grins. Quick cuts: three more cars lined up on the street, a new yellow pressure washer, Daniel (now about 21) writing in a notebook labelled EXPENSES, then shaking hands with his first employee (who wears the same red) in front of a white van with a red stripe.
**Camera:** low side angle on the washing, a high wide shot of the line of cars, a medium two-shot for the handshake.
**SFX:** water spray, a cash register, "pop" sounds for each milestone.
**On-screen:** "ONE CUSTOMER → MORE OPPORTUNITIES", then chips: SAVE SOME · REINVEST · MORE CUSTOMERS · BETTER ADS · TRACK EXPENSES · 1ST EMPLOYEE.

### Scene 5 (38–48 s): Ten years of consistency
**Narration:** "Of course, some businesses fail, and investments can lose money. But ten years of learning, saving, and improving can give you opportunities you might never have had otherwise."
**Prompt:** Rain pours on the driveway; a customer's car drives away, the bucket tips over, and DANIEL (22) stands frustrated in the rain. Then a fast montage in a studio: a calendar flips YEAR 1 → YEAR 10 while Daniel ages from 18 to 28 (a beard grows, the hoodie becomes a work jacket). Beside him, a small investment bar chart dips red in a downturn (years 4–5) but a coin keeps dropping in every month and the bars turn green and grow again.
**Camera:** handheld-feel close-up in the rain, then a slow push-in on Daniel during the time-lapse.
**SFX:** rain, a low thud, page flips, coin clinks.
**On-screen:** "10 YEARS OF SMALL DECISIONS", "RAINED OUT", "LOST A CUSTOMER", "AGE n", "MARKET DOWN, STILL +$100/MO".

### Scene 6 (48–60 s): The final message
**Narration:** "You don't have to become a millionaire to change your life. Having savings, useful skills, and more choices is already success. Your future won't build itself. What small step could you take today?"
**Prompt:** Sunrise. DANIEL (28) stands outside his modest shop with a red sign and a white van with a red stripe parked nearby. He holds a framed photo of himself at 18 in the red hoodie, smiles softly, lowers it, and looks toward the city. His employee waves from the doorway. The camera slowly pulls back and up to reveal the skyline glowing orange in the sunrise.
**Camera:** medium close-up on Daniel and the photo, then a slow crane pull-back to a wide aerial.
**SFX:** music swell, a gentle whoosh on the pull-back, a final cinematic boom on "START TODAY".
**On-screen:** "START SMALL. STAY CONSISTENT. THINK LONG-TERM.", then a big "START TODAY".
